# FRLG Completion Dex

A phone-friendly, offline-capable 100% completion guide for Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen, inspired by RouteDex.

- **Routes**: all 90 Kanto and Sevii Islands locations in story order. Each one has walkthrough steps, every trainer in the order you meet them (party, levels, HP, moves, weaknesses, prize money and rewards, with a damage estimate for each of your Pokémon against each of theirs), wild Pokémon (method, levels, encounter rate, FR/LG-only tags), items on the ground, hidden items, NPC gifts and in-game trades, all as checklists.
- **Story progress**: fishing, Surf and Rock Smash encounters are listed under "come back later" until you have the rod or HM. When your team has no super-effective answer to a trainer's Pokémon, the app suggests one you could have caught by that point.
- **Pokédex**: Kanto 151 or National 386, showing where to catch each one, what to evolve it from, or which version to trade from.
- **Battle plan**: enter your team to see who to send out against each Gym Leader, rival battle, Giovanni, the Elite Four and the Champion (rival teams depend on your starter).
- **Team plan**: the best team at every point of the game for your version and starter. Before each key battle it lists who to catch or box, who evolves, which moves and TMs to teach, the lineup and who to lead with, all as checkboxes. The battle checkbox is the same one as on the route page, and a button copies the lineup into the Battle plan.
- **Competitive builds**: every Pokémon page lists Smogon's ADV (Gen 3) sets: best natures, abilities, items and top moves at a glance, then each full build (tier, item, ability, nature, EV spread, IVs and Hidden Power, moves with alternatives). Every move and item says how to get it in FireRed/LeafGreen (level, TM, tutor, egg move) or flags it ⚠ when it needs another game or an event. A button copies the set in Showdown format, and a link opens Smogon's analysis. The 85 Pokémon Smogon has no set for (mostly unevolved ones) get a hand-written Draft build.
- **Reference pages**: every Pokémon (stats, abilities, catch rate, EV yield, where to get it, evolutions, level-up/TM/tutor moves), TM, item and move, plus search (🔍) across all of them.
- **Progress**: completion by category and part of the game, badges, "still missing" Pokémon with the earliest place to get each, and a tappable town map shaded by completion.
- **Shops and tutors**: buy-only TMs and Game Corner prizes as checklist rows; every move tutor on its location page.
- **Item pages**: tap any item, gift or shop item for the game's own description, what a held berry does, which Pokémon an evolution stone evolves, every shop that sells it (in story order), Game Corner prizes, and every place to find it.
- **Catch plan**: tap "Catch plan" next to any wild Pokémon on a route (or a catch step in the Team plan) for the odds with every ball the shops sell by that point in the story, and a step-by-step plan from your team's moves: False Swipe, sleep or paralysis, the strongest hit that can't knock it out, plus warnings (Teleport, Selfdestruct, Roar, Static...) and Pokémon you could catch to help. The Master Ball is never planned for.
- **Tools**: breeding helper, type matchups, version exclusives, TM list, stats/IV calculator, catch odds, level forecast, EV training and counter, one-time Pokémon, move tutors, Unown forms, Sevii story checklist, Nuzlocke run tracker (level cap, dupes clause), shiny hunt counters, living dex boxes, and .sav import (ticks caught Pokémon, items and trainers, and loads your party).
- Separate FireRed and LeafGreen saves, stored on the device, with backup export and import.
- Optional cloud sync between devices keyed by an email address (no password). Needs the small Cloudflare Worker in `worker/`; see `worker/README.md`.

Plain HTML, CSS and JS with no build step. Host the folder on any static host (GitHub Pages works).
Run locally with `python3 -m http.server` in this folder, then open http://localhost:8000.

## Data

`data/` is generated by `scripts/build_data.py` from the [pret/pokefirered](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered) decompilation,
so encounter tables, item placements, NPC gifts, trades and trainer parties match the game itself:

```
git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/pret/pokefirered /tmp/pokefirered
python3 -I scripts/build_data.py /tmp/pokefirered data
```

Items are split by when you can reach them. `scripts/reach.py` (the same file as in the Emerald app) walks the real map tiles: water, waterfalls, ledges, Cut trees, Rock Smash rocks, Strength boulders, Snorlax, warps and the Seagallop ferries, with each map opening when the story reaches its location and each field move once you have its HM and badge (`ABILITIES` in the build script). An item that needs a later point than the place's first visit is listed under "Items: come back later" with what it needs. `tests/item_reach.js` checks the result against every Bulbapedia walkthrough note that says an item "requires" a field move.

`data/walkthrough.json` holds the hand-written story steps and is not overwritten by the script.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `locations.json` | Story-order list of locations with their section |
| `locations/<id>.json` | `encounters[]` (dex, name, method, versions, levels, rate, area, notes), `items[]` (id, name, hidden, where), `gifts[]` (id, name, from, where, versions?), `trainers[]` (id, class, name, prize, party or byStarter, rewards?, uses?, double?, area?, stage?) |
| `pokedex.json` | 386 species: types, base stats, abilities, catch rate, EXP yield, growth rate, EV yield, wild held items, egg groups, gender ratio, egg cycles, evolutions, learnsets, TM/HM and tutor compatibility |
| `trainers.json` | Key battles for the Battle plan tab (Gym Leaders, rivals, Giovanni, Elite Four, Champion), same shape as route trainers |
| `types.json` | Gen 3 type chart (attacker → defender → multiplier) |
| `moves.json` | Move name → type, power and special damage effect, for the damage estimates |
| `extras.json` | Shops (stock and prices), move tutors (where, who can learn), VS Seeker rematch teams, Unown letters per Tanoby chamber |
| `save-map.json` | For .sav import: the save flag behind each item and trainer row, internal species and move numbers, badge flags |
| `items.json` | Every item's Bag description, price and pocket, plus what held berries do |
| `region-map.json` | The game's town-map grid per region, cell → location |
| `tms.json` | TM/HM number → move, whether it can be reused (HMs, Department Store and Game Corner TMs) and every place you get one; `pokedex.json` lists each species' `tmhm` |
| `builds.json` | Competitive builds per national dex number (shared with the Emerald app): Smogon ADV sets (format, name, level, move slots, items, abilities, natures, EVs, IVs, Hidden Power IVs) and how each move is learned in Gen 3. Built by `scripts/build_builds.py`, see below |

## Competitive builds

`data/builds.json` comes from Smogon's Gen 3 strategy dex (as published by [pkmn/smogon](https://github.com/pkmn/smogon)) and is checked against
[Pokémon Showdown](https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown)'s Gen 3 learnsets, so every move is one the Pokémon (or what it evolves from) can learn in Gen 3.
Pokémon without a Smogon set get the hand-written Draft builds in `scripts/draft_builds.txt`, which go through the same checks.

```
curl -sLo /tmp/gen3.json https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pkmn/smogon/main/data/sets/gen3.json
git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown /tmp/ps
python3 -I scripts/build_builds.py /tmp/gen3.json /tmp/ps data
```

`node tests/builds.js` (in CI) checks every set renders on its Pokémon page, every move resolves to a way to learn it, and items, abilities, natures, EVs and Hidden Power IVs are legal.

## Team planner

`node scripts/team_planner.js --out docs/team-guide.md` scores candidate teams against every trainer up to the Champion with the Battle plan's damage engine (plus TMs and HMs, with single-use TMs handed to one member) and writes the most viable six for each version and starter, with where to get each member, evolutions and moves over the game. The model and scoring are described at the top of the script.
Add `--stages-out docs/team-stages.md` for the stage-by-stage version: before every key battle, who to catch, box, evolve and what to teach, with temporary members filling slots until the end-game six can be caught.
Add `--app-json data/team-plan.json` to refresh the Team plan page's data (run all three together after any data or engine change; it takes about a minute).

## Tests

`node tests/audit_battles.js` (Node 18+, no installs) loads `app.js` with a stub DOM and audits the battle picks: for every trainer in the game (route, cave, building and Sevii trainers as well as
Gym Leaders, rivals, Giovanni, the Elite Four and the Champion), in both versions and with every starter, it checks each catch suggestion is
catchable by then, evolves legally, only uses moves it can really know, and is super effective; it fails when a
brute-force search finds a super-effective catch the planner missed. It also checks the type chart against Gen 3, key move
data, and known answers (Pikachu, Oddish or Bellsprout for Misty; Diglett for Lt. Surge). It also checks trainers behind Surf, Cut, the Poké Flute or the post-game aren't planned before you can reach them. Add `--report out.txt` to dump every
suggestion. GitHub Actions runs it on every push and pull request.

`node tests/team_plan.js` checks `data/team-plan.json` and the Team plan page: legs in story order with levels that never drop, every catch reachable by then, legal evolutions and moves, TMs in hand by then and single-use TMs taught once, real battles, lead picks re-run through the damage engine, and the page's checkboxes saving and reloading. CI runs it too.

`node tests/walkthrough.js` checks that every place has walkthrough steps, that every `{gift:}`, `{trainer:}`, `{loc:}`, `{mon:}` and `{item:}` link in a step points at real data on that page, and that pages render cleanly. Steps live in `data/walkthrough.json`; places in its `_checked` map were checked against the pokefirered event scripts, and the rest show a Draft tag.

Progress keys are `dex:<number>` for Pokémon and `<location-id>:item:<id>` or `<location-id>:gift:<id>` for checklists, so keep ids stable.
